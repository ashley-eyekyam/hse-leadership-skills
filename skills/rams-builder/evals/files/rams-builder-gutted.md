# RAMS — Replace South-Elevation Cladding Panels, Levels 2–4

**Client role:** Principal Contractor (CDM 2015) · **Site:** occupied building, south elevation

## Method — sequence of works

1. Mobilise to site and sign in.
2. Inspect and erect the mobile access tower.
3. Transfer materials and tools to the working level with the MEWP.
4. Remove the old cladding panels.
5. Fit the new cladding panels.
6. Inspect the completed elevation.
7. Dismantle the tower.
8. Clear the site and hand back.

## Risk rating

| Activity | Rating | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| Working from the tower | High | Tower erected by a trained operative; guardrails fitted; wear a harness |
| Panel handling | Medium | Two-person lift; wear gloves |
| MEWP transfer | Medium | Trained operator; wear a harness |

## Competencies (as supplied)

- Tower erection: **PASMA** card holder.
- MEWP operation: **IPAF** card holder.
