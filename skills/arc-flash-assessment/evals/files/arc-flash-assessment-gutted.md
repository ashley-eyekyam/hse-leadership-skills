# Arc Flash Assessment — Switchboard

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Equipment

The assessment covers the main low-voltage switchboard where maintenance is carried out.

## Assessment

An arc flash could occur during work on the energised switchboard. To protect the worker,
arc-rated PPE is provided and must be worn during the task. The PPE is selected to give
protection against the expected level of arc flash.

## Controls

- Wear the arc-rated PPE provided for all work on the board.
- Only trained electricians carry out the work.
- Keep the panel doors closed where possible.
