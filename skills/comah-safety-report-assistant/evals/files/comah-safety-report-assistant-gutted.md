## 1. Introduction

This Safety Report covers the Site-X terminal, an upper-tier establishment storing LPG in
mounded bullets and a sphere, and petrol in floating-roof tanks. The dangerous substances
held on site place the establishment in the upper tier, and this report presents the
establishment's arrangements for the prevention of major accidents.

## 2. Major Accident Prevention Policy (MAPP)

The operator is committed to preventing major accidents and to limiting their
consequences for people and the environment. Safety is managed as a core business value,
and the arrangements described in this report give effect to that commitment across the
terminal's operations.

## 3. Safety management system

The terminal operates a safety management system covering operating procedures, permit
to work, management of change, competence and training, and audit. The system is
maintained by the site management team and is subject to periodic internal review.

## 4. Establishment description

The terminal comprises the LPG storage area (two mounded bullets and one sphere), the
petrol tank farm (three floating-roof tanks), the road-tanker loading gantries and the
associated pipework, utilities and control room. The site occupies a level, fenced
compound accessed from the main road, with the office and workshop buildings at the
front of the site.

## 5. Major-accident scenarios

The principal scenarios for the establishment are a BLEVE of the LPG sphere under fire
impingement, a vapour-cloud explosion following a transfer-line failure, a tank-top fire
on a petrol tank, and a large petrol release. The likelihood of each scenario is low,
and the consequence assessment shows that the risk from the establishment's operations
is acceptable. Individual risk at the fence line is 3 × 10⁻⁶ per year.

## 6. Risk-reduction position

The establishment's risks are as low as reasonably practicable (ALARP). The protective
measures in place — mounding of the bullets, water deluge on the sphere, rim-seal foam
pourers, and overfill protection — reflect good practice for a terminal of this type,
and no further measures are considered necessary.

## 7. Emergency arrangements

Emergency plans are in place for the establishment and are exercised with the fire and
rescue service. The plans cover the response to incidents at the LPG and petrol storage
areas and the arrangements for informing the authorities.
