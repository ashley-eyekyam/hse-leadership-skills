# Dropped Objects — Site Note

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Overview

Work is taking place at height above walkways and work areas. This note covers the arrangements
to protect people below from dropped objects.

## Controls

- All personnel below the work area must wear hard hats at all times.
- Tools used at height should be tethered where practicable.
- A register of tools taken to height is kept by the supervisor.
- Barriers are placed at ground level where possible.

## Review

The arrangements will be reviewed by the site team as work progresses.
