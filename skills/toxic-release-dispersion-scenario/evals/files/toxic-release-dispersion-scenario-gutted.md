# Toxic-Release Dispersion Scenario — Chlorine

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Facility:** chlorination building.

## 1. Substance & source term
- Substance: chlorine (CAS 7782-50-5).
- Inventory at risk: 1 tonne in a liquefied-gas drum at ambient temperature.
- Release mode: catastrophic full-bore failure of the drum.

## 2. Weather
The prevailing wind is south-westerly at 3 m/s with Pasquill stability class D.

## 3. Dispersion result
For the catastrophic release, the chlorine cloud disperses downwind and reaches the AEGL-2
threshold at approximately 1.2 km and the AEGL-3 threshold at approximately 450 m from the release
point under the stated weather. The nearest residential receptor, 300 m to the NE, therefore lies
within the AEGL-3 zone.

## 4. Receptors
The receptors are the on-site workers and the residential edge 300 m to the NE.

## 5. Consequence
Given the AEGL-3 distance reaches the residential edge, the consequence severity is rated **Major**
on the site's 5x5 matrix.

## 6. Actions
| Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Review the chlorine drum storage arrangement | Process Safety Lead (role) | 12 July 2026 |
| Confirm the site alarm covers the residential edge | Emergency Planning Lead (role) | 18 July 2026 |
