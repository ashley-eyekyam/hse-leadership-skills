# Emergency Response Plan — Plant 4 Solvent-Storage Warehouse

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026
**Site:** Plant 4 solvent-storage warehouse (45 staff, 2 shifts)

---

## 1. Scenarios

The credible emergencies for this warehouse are a fire or explosion involving drummed flammable
solvent, and a chemical spill. This plan sets out how the site responds to each.

## 2. Response procedures

On discovering a fire, staff raise the alarm and evacuate the building. The fire is tackled with
extinguishers only if it is safe to do so. For a chemical spill, staff withdraw from the area,
raise the alarm, and contain the spill where safe using the spill kit.

## 3. Assembly

On the alarm, everyone leaves the building and assembles at the car park. A supervisor checks
who is present.

## 4. Roles

The Site Manager (role) takes charge of the response and liaises with the emergency services.
Wardens sweep the building and report to the Site Manager.

## 5. Drills

The site runs evacuation drills to keep staff familiar with the procedure.
