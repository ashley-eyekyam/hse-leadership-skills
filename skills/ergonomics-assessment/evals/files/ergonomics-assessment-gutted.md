# Ergonomics Assessment — Despatch-bay carton handling

*Decision-support only. A competent person should review this assessment.*

## 1. Task / workstation description
- **Task:** despatch-bay carton lift — cartons are lifted from a floor pallet onto the conveyor
  infeed.
- **Role / SEG:** the line-2 packer SEG.
- **Method:** the assessment used the NIOSH lifting-equation approach together with a RULA posture
  observation of the packers at the infeed.

## 2. Observations
The packers bend to floor level to pick up the cartons and twist to place them onto the conveyor.
The cartons are moderately heavy and are handled frequently through the shift. On observation the
posture and the repetition suggest an elevated manual-handling risk to the lower back for the
packer role.

## 3. Risk rating
Overall this task is assessed as a **high manual-handling risk** for the lower back, driven by the
floor-level origin, the twist, and the lifting frequency across the shift.

## 4. Controls
- Deliver manual-handling training to all packers and refresh it annually.
- Brief the packers on good lifting technique — bend the knees, keep the load close, avoid the
  twist.
- Supervisors to remind the team to take short breaks and to ask for help with heavier cartons.
- Hi-vis and safety footwear worn in the despatch bay.
