## 1. Applicant and route

This application is made by the Midland metro infrastructure manager for a safety
authorisation under ROGS. As the infrastructure manager for the metro network, the
applicant is responsible for the safe management and operation of the network's
infrastructure, and this pack is assembled for submission to ORR accordingly.

## 2. Safety management arrangements

The applicant operates a Safety Management System covering the management of the
network's infrastructure. The SMS provides for safety policy, the management of risk,
the competence of staff, maintenance of assets, emergency arrangements, and monitoring
and audit, and these arrangements are summarised in the sections below.

### 2.1 Policy and organisation

The safety policy commits the organisation to the safe operation of the network. The
management structure assigns safety responsibilities across the operations and
engineering directorates.

### 2.2 Risk management

Risks are identified and assessed, and control measures are maintained through the asset
management and operational planning processes. The risk register is reviewed on a
periodic cycle.

### 2.3 Maintenance

Infrastructure assets are maintained in accordance with the maintenance regime, with
inspection frequencies set by asset type and condition monitored through the asset
management system.

### 2.4 Change

Changes to the infrastructure are managed through the engineering change process, which
provides for the assessment of changes before they are brought into use.

### 2.5 Emergency and monitoring

Emergency plans cover the network's principal scenarios, and the SMS is subject to
internal audit with findings reported to the executive.

## 3. Supporting information

The application is supported by the SMS documentation summarised above, which is
available to ORR on request. The applicant considers the arrangements described to be
appropriate to the scale and nature of the network's operations.
