# Job Safety Analysis — Clean & Inspect Tank T-402, Plant 3

**Task:** Clean and inspect storage tank T-402 · **Crew:** own workers + contractors

| # | Step | Hazard | Risk | Controls | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up at the tank and prepare equipment | Slips, trips around the work area | Medium | Keep the area tidy; wear safety boots | Team Lead |
| 2 | Open the tank and ventilate | Vapours in the confined space | High | Run the blower before anyone approaches; wear a respirator | Team Lead |
| 3 | Enter the tank | Poor visibility, tight access | High | Use the cherry-picker; wear a harness and helmet | Entrant |
| 4 | Clean the interior with solvent | Solvent splashes to skin/eyes | Medium | Wear chemical gloves and goggles | Entrant |
| 5 | Inspect and record findings | Manual-handling of tools | Low | Follow good handling technique | Inspector |
| 6 | Exit and restore the tank | Trip on the manway | Medium | Take care stepping out; wear gloves | Entrant |

## Notes
- All crew to attend a pre-task briefing.
- PPE for the job: coveralls, boots, gloves, goggles, respirator, harness.

*Decision-support only; a competent person must review before use.*
