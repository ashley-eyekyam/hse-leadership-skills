# Secondary Containment Review — Fuel-Depot Tank Farm

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Site:** fuel-depot tank farm, shared-bund group.

## 1. Tanks in the bund
| Tank | Product | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| T-1 | Diesel | 500 m3 |
| T-2 | Diesel | 500 m3 |
| T-3 | Gasoline | 750 m3 |

The three tanks share a single common bund.

## 2. Containment capacity
The bund provides a net capacity of 620 m3, which is considered adequate to contain a release
from the tank farm. The bund walls are of reinforced concrete construction.

## 3. Segregation
Gasoline and diesel are broadly compatible, so no additional segregation between the tanks is
required within the shared bund. The tanks are spaced to allow access for inspection.

## 4. Bund integrity
- The bund wall and floor are inspected for cracks on a routine basis.
- Pipe penetrations through the bund wall are sealed and checked during inspection.
- The bund is kept clear of accumulated rainwater.

## 5. Operational controls
- Tank levels are monitored by the site gauging system and checked before each delivery.
- Deliveries are supervised by a trained operator who watches the level during filling.
- No smoking and no naked flames are permitted in the tank-farm area.
