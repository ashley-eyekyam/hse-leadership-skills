# Safety Culture Assessment — Northgate Distribution Centre

**Northgate DC** · 5 July 2026

## 1. Introduction

This assessment reports the safety-culture maturity of the Northgate distribution centre. It
uses the **Bradley** Curve as its **model**, placing the site on the recognised four-stage
maturity scale.

## 2. Method

A safety-climate pulse survey was issued to staff at the site. The responses were analysed to
gauge attitudes to safety, management commitment, and reporting confidence, and to place the
site on the Bradley scale.

## 3. Findings

The survey indicates that the site sits at the **"Dependent"** stage of the Bradley Curve.
Staff generally follow the rules set for them, and safety is seen as driven by supervision
rather than owned personally across the site.

Findings are reported at the level of the site and by broad **role** group (operations, drivers,
warehouse) so that patterns can be seen.

## 4. Advancement

To advance toward the "Independent" and "Interdependent" stages, the site should:

- Increase the visibility of leaders on the floor
- Encourage more open safety conversations
- Run a safety-awareness campaign to raise personal ownership of safety

## 5. Conclusion

The Northgate site is assessed at the Dependent stage on the Bradley model, with scope to
advance through the actions above.
