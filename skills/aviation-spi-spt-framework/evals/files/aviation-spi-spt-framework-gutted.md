# Safety Performance Indicator (SPI) Framework

**DGCA-certificated A320 scheduled operator** · Indicator set · 5 July 2026

## 1. Purpose

This framework sets out the safety performance indicators the operator will track for its
priority **hazards** — unstabilised approaches and runway excursions — in support of its safety
**objectives**.

## 2. Indicators

We will track a mix of **leading** and **lagging** indicators:

**Leading indicators**
- Rate of unstabilised-approach FDM events per month
- Proportion of approaches meeting the stabilised-approach gate
- Go-around compliance rate

**Lagging indicators**
- Runway-excursion events per quarter
- Hard-landing events per quarter

## 3. Measurement

Each indicator is measured on a monthly or quarterly **period** basis, drawn from the
operator's flight data and occurrence records. The indicators map to the priority hazards in
the operator's hazard register.

## 4. Use

The indicators will give the operator visibility of its safety performance against its priority
hazards and objectives over time.
