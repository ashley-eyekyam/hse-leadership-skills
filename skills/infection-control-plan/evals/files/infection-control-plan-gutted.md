# Infection Control Plan — Inpatient Unit, Site-D

**Document date:** 5 July 2026
**Prepared for:** Ward Manager (role)

## 1. Standard precautions

Standard precautions apply to every patient regardless of known status — hand hygiene at the point of care, personal protective equipment matched to the anticipated exposure, respiratory hygiene, and safe injection practice.

## 2. Transmission-based precautions

Additional **transmission-based** precautions are selected by the route of spread:

- **Contact** — single room or cohorting, gown and gloves, dedicated equipment.
- **Droplet** — single room or spatial separation, surgical mask within the patient zone.
- **Airborne** — the patient is placed in a single room with the door closed and a respirator is worn on entry.

## 3. Administrative controls

- **Cohort** patients with the same organism where single rooms are limited.
- **Hand hygiene** audits on the unit each month.
- Early **screening** and **signage** at the room entrance to communicate the precautions in force.

## 4. Notes

Staff are reminded of the precautions at the daily safety huddle.
