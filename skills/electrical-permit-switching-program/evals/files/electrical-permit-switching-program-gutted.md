# Switching Program — Feeder Isolation

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Task

Isolate the feeder to allow maintenance on the downstream panel.

## Sequence

- Open the feeder circuit breaker.
- Confirm the panel is off.
- Apply a lock and tag to the breaker.
- Carry out the maintenance work.
- On completion, remove the lock and close the breaker.

## Authorisation

The switching is carried out by a competent electrician and signed off by the supervisor.
