# Warehouse Racking & MHE Safety Assessment — Coventry DC

**Coventry DC · APR pallet racking, bays A1–A48** · 5 July 2026

## 1. Scope

This assessment covers the pallet racking installation and the mechanical-handling operation at
the Coventry distribution centre, bays A1–A48.

## 2. Racking

The racking is adjustable pallet racking. The rack uprights carry **column protection** at the
aisle ends and floor level, and the racking is loaded within its **SWL** as understood by the
operations team. The installation is inspected as part of the site's routine safety rounds.

## 3. Damage findings

A visual look around the racking identified some damage:

| Location | Damage | Band |
|----------|--------|------|
| Bay A12, upright | Impact scuff, minor deformation | **Amber** |
| Bay A31, beam | Light scuffing | Green |
| Bay A44, upright | Impact deformation | **Red** |

The Amber and Red items are logged for the maintenance team to look at.

## 4. MHE and traffic

The operation runs reach trucks and powered pallet trucks in the aisles. **Pedestrian** access
to the pick-aisles is managed, and staff are reminded to be aware of moving vehicles through
signage and hi-vis clothing.

## 5. Conclusion

The racking and traffic operation at Coventry DC is broadly in order, with the damage items
above to be followed up.
