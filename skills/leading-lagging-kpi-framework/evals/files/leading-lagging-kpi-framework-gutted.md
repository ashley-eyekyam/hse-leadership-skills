# HSE KPI Framework — Northgate Operations

**Northgate Operations · FY2026** · 5 July 2026

## Purpose

This framework sets out the health-and-safety key performance indicators Northgate Operations
will track through the year, combining **leading** and **lagging** measures to give a rounded
view of safety performance.

## Leading indicators

- Percentage of planned inspections completed
- Near-miss reporting rate
- Safety-training completion
- Toolbox-talk delivery

## Lagging indicators

- **TRIR** (Total Recordable **Incident Rate**)
- **LTIFR** (Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate)
- Days lost to injury

## Reporting

Each indicator is reported monthly on the operations safety dashboard with a red/amber/green
status, so management can see at a glance where performance is on or off track.

## Targets

Targets are set for each indicator at the start of the year, drawn from the previous year's
figures, and progress is tracked against them through the period.
