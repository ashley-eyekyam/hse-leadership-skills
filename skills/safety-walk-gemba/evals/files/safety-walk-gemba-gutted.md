# Safety Leadership Walk — Night-Shift Warehouse Pick-Face

**Night-shift pick-face** · 5 July 2026

## Purpose

This is a safety leadership walk on the night-shift warehouse pick-face. It is about
**engagement** — a **conversation** with the crew about how the work feels and what could make
it safer, framed against **ISO 45001** **clause 5.1** (leadership and commitment).

## Prompts for the walk

The walk leader uses **open** questions to start the conversation, for example:

- "Talk me through how the pick-face is running tonight."
- "What gets in the way of doing this job safely?"
- "If you could change one thing about this area, what would it be?"

The intent is to listen to the crew's own account, not to inspect against a checklist.

## On the walk

The walk leader spends time on the pick-face, talks with the pickers, and notes the themes that
come up. The conversation is kept supportive and non-judgemental so people feel able to speak
openly.

## After the walk

The themes raised are shared with the shift management team so that the points made by the crew
are heard and can be considered.
