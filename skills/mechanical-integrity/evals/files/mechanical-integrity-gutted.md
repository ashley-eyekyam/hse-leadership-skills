## 3. Testing arrangements

Relief valves are removed and bench tested on a two-year cycle by the site maintenance
workshop, with results recorded in the maintenance management system. Valves found
outside tolerance are refurbished and re-tested before return to service. The population
above is currently within its test cycle and the unit's relief protection is considered
to be in good order.

## 4. Programme summary

The two-year testing cycle provides a consistent and manageable programme for the
population, aligned with the site's maintenance planning calendar. The programme will
continue on the current cycle, and the register above will be updated as each test is
completed.
