## 1. Similar-exposure groups (SEG)
| SEG | Operators | Agents | Existing control |
|---|---|---|---|

## 2. Exposure limits
Each agent is banded against its occupational exposure limit (OEL):

- Toluene — 50 ppm (8-hour average).
- Xylene — 50 ppm (8-hour average).

## 3. Exposure banding
| SEG × agent | Exposure basis | Band |
|---|---|---|
| Decanting × Toluene | 8-hour average | Medium |
| Blending × Xylene | 8-hour average | Low |

Both SEGs are assessed against the 8-hour average exposure limit for their solvent.

## 4. Controls
- Operators in both SEGs are issued organic-vapour RPE and instructed to wear it during solvent
  handling.
- RPE is face-fit tested and maintained on the RPE programme.
- Solvent containers are kept closed when not in use and stored in the flammables store.

## 5. Monitoring

| Operator 3 | Blending | within limit | Low |
