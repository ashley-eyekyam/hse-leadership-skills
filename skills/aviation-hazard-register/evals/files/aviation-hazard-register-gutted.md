# Hazard Register — Bird Strike on Departure

**DGCA-certificated A320 scheduled operator** · Register entry · 5 July 2026

## Entry HR-014 — Bird strike on departure

**Hazard.** Bird strike to the aircraft during the departure phase.

**Consequence.** Credible worst consequence is engine damage or windshield damage during a
critical phase of flight, with potential for a rejected take-off or an air-return.

**Existing controls.** The aerodrome operates a wildlife-management programme, and crews are
briefed to be alert to bird activity at the departure aerodrome during known migratory periods.

**Classification.**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Severity | Hazardous |
| Likelihood | Occasional |
| Risk rating (5×5) | High |

The rating reflects the credible severity of an engine or windshield strike in the departure
phase combined with the assessed likelihood at this aerodrome.

**Evidence.** The hazard is supported by the aerodrome's occurrence history of bird activity
during the migratory season and by the fleet's own reports.

## Notes

This entry will be maintained in the operator's hazard register and reviewed at the next Safety
Review Board.
