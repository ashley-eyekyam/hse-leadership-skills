# Business Continuity Plan — Claims Processing, Northgate Operations Centre

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## 1. Scope

This plan sets out how the claims-processing function at the Northgate Operations Centre will
maintain and recover its critical activities in the event of a disruption. The critical
activities are claims intake and validation, the claims payment run, and the customer contact
centre.

## 2. Continuity strategies

For each critical activity a continuity strategy is defined against its key dependencies:

| Dependency | Continuity strategy |
|---|---|
| People | Cross-trained staff and a call-in roster for surge cover |
| IT systems | Failover to the secondary application instance |
| Premises | Relocation to the Southgate site or home working |
| Suppliers | Backup print/mail supplier on standby |

Each strategy is designed to keep the critical activity running or restore it quickly after a
disruption.

## 3. Recovery roles

The recovery effort is led by a Recovery Manager (role) supported by function leads for claims,
IT and facilities. The roles co-ordinate the response and report on recovery progress.

## 4. Testing

The plan will be tested on a schedule to confirm the strategies work. A tabletop test schedule
is maintained by the business continuity team.

## 5. Review

The plan is reviewed annually and after any invocation.
