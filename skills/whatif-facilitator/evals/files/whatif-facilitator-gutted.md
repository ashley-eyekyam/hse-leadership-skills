## 1. Scope

This review covers the road-tanker loading procedure at Gantry 2, Site-X, from tanker
arrival and positioning through loading to departure checks. The review considered the
loading operation as described in the current procedure.

## 2. Review record

| # | Question | Discussion | Safeguards |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What if the wrong product is loaded? | Product crossover would contaminate the delivery and could create a reactive or flammable hazard at the customer site | Operator checks the loading order against the tanker placard before connecting |
| 2 | What if the tanker moves off during loading? | A drive-off with the loading arm connected would rupture the arm and release product at the gantry | Wheel chocks; keys held at the loading panel |
| 3 | What if the tanker is overfilled? | Overfill would release product from the tanker vents onto the gantry area | Preset meter quantity; operator attendance during loading |
| 4 | What if the loading arm coupling leaks? | A coupling leak would release product at the connection point during transfer | Visual check of the coupling on connection |
| 5 | What if a fire starts at the gantry during loading? | A gantry fire during transfer would threaten the connected tanker and the loading bay | Emergency stop at the gantry; extinguishers at the bay |

## 3. Summary

The review walked the loading operation question by question and recorded the safeguards
in place for each concern raised. The loading procedure was found to address the
principal concerns at the gantry, and the safeguards recorded above reflect the current
arrangements for the operation.

The review record has been filed with the procedure owner for reference at the next
procedure revision.
