# Lone Working Assessment — Mobile Technician

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Activity

A service technician visits customer sites alone to carry out routine equipment servicing.

## Assessment

Working alone carries the risk that, if the technician has an accident or feels unwell, no one is
immediately on hand to help.

## Controls

- The technician carries a mobile phone at all times.
- The technician texts the office when arriving at and leaving each site.
- The office keeps a copy of the day's schedule.
