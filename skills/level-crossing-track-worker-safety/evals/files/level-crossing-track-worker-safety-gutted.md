## 1. Crossing description

Mill Lane is a passive footpath crossing on a double-track section carrying mixed
passenger and freight traffic. The crossing serves a public right of way linking the
village to the riverside path and sees regular pedestrian use, with peaks at school and
commuting times.

## 2. Risk review

The assessment walked the crossing environment on both approaches. Sighting from the
decision point is affected by vegetation growth on the down-side approach, and the
existing signage is weathered. Pedestrian behaviour observed during the visit was
generally compliant, though users with pushchairs and dogs take longer to clear the
crossing than the sighting time comfortably allows.

## 3. Improvement plan

| # | Improvement |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cut back vegetation on the down-side approach to restore sighting distance |
| 2 | Renew both crossing information signs and the emergency contact plate |
| 3 | Refresh the deck surface and approach markings |
| 4 | Add a "STOP, LOOK, LISTEN" reminder sign at each gate |
| 5 | Include the crossing in the seasonal vegetation maintenance round |

The improvement plan addresses the issues observed at the crossing and will restore the
crossing environment to good order. The works are within the routine maintenance
programme and can be delivered in the current period.

## 4. Site-safety arrangements

Works at the crossing will be carried out under the maintainer's standard arrangements
for working at passive crossings, with the local maintenance team delivering the
vegetation and signage works.
