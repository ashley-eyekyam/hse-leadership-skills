# Mine-Rescue Emergency Response Plan — Underground Coal Mine

## 1. Command structure

In an emergency, the Mine Manager is the Incident Controller and directs the response. The
Control Room Operator handles notification and calls out the team. A clear chain of command
is maintained throughout.

## 2. Notification

On an alarm, the Control Room Operator will notify the Incident Controller, the on-site
team, and the external authorities per the call-out list.

## 3. Mutual aid

The mine has a mutual-aid link with the district rescue station. The rescue station provides
additional rescuers and apparatus when called.

## 4. Drills

| Drill | Owner | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Call-out drill | Control Room Operator | Quarterly |
| Team turnout drill | Rescue Team Captain | Half-yearly |

Drills are logged and reviewed by the Mine Manager.
