# Regulatory Return — Workplace Injury

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## The incident

A worker sustained an injury at the facility and required medical treatment beyond first aid.
The event has been logged in the internal incident system.

## Assessment

Based on the evidence in the incident report, this determination is that the injury appears recordable. It should be entered on the injury log and included in
the annual summary of workplace injuries.

## Action

- Record the injury on the injury log.
- Include it in the annual injury summary.
- Notify the safety manager.

The organisation should keep its injury records up to date and submit the annual summary as
required.
