# Lift Plan — packaged chiller to the level-4 roof, Northgate site

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Jurisdiction:** UK — LOLER 1998 / BS 7121.

## 1. Lift classification
This is a **standard lift** under BS 7121: a single mobile crane placing a packaged chiller onto
the level-4 roof deck. A documented lift plan is provided and the crane supervisor will oversee
the operation on the day.

## 2. Load
The packaged chiller is approximately 12 tonnes including the lifting frame. Overall dimensions
are about 4.0 × 2.0 × 2.3 m, with four lifting eyes and the centre of gravity marked on the unit.

## 3. Crane & capacity
A 50 t mobile crane on outriggers will be used, working at roughly 12 m radius with the boom
extended. The crane's safe working load at this radius comfortably exceeds the load, so the lift
is well within the machine's capacity. Outrigger mats will be laid on the hardstanding.

## 4. Site & proximity
- A power line runs along the site boundary; operatives are to take care and keep clear of it
  during the lift.
- The set-down area on the roof deck will be cleared of loose materials before the lift.

## 5. Sequenced lift method
1. **Rig** the chiller to the four lifting eyes and check the accessory certificates.
2. **Trial-lift** the load clear of the ground to confirm stability.
3. **Travel / slew** the load to the roof under the direction of the slinger / signaller.
4. **Place** the chiller on the roof set-down point.
5. **De-rig** and clear the area.

## 6. Roles
| Role | On the day |
|---|---|
| Crane Supervisor | Oversees the lift |
| Crane Operator | Operates the crane |
| Slinger / Signaller | Attaches the load and signals |

## 7. Weather
The lift will not proceed in high winds or poor visibility; the supervisor will make the call on
the day.

## 8. Sign-off
Prepared as decision-support for the project team; this lift plan should be reviewed by a
competent person before the lift.
