# Confidential Safety Reporting Scheme

**DGCA-certificated A320 scheduled passenger operator** · Scheme design · 5 July 2026

## 1. Purpose

This scheme provides a **confidential** channel through which staff can raise safety concerns.
It complements the operator's wider safety management system and supports an open safety
culture in which people feel able to speak up.

## 2. Scheme type

The scheme is a **confidential** reporting scheme: reports are received and handled by the
safety team. The scheme sits alongside the operator's other safety reporting arrangements.

## 3. How to report

Reports may be submitted through the safety portal, by paper form, or by the safety hotline.
Reporters are encouraged to provide as much detail as possible so that the concern can be
properly understood and acted upon.

## 4. Handling

The safety team reviews each report, considers the safety issue raised, and decides what
action is appropriate. Where a wider safety lesson arises, it is shared across the operator so
that the whole organisation benefits.

## 5. Feedback

Reporters will receive **feedback** on the **outcome** of their report. General safety lessons
are published in the periodic safety **bulletin** so that the workforce can see that reporting
leads to action.

## 6. Protection basis

The scheme is operated consistent with the protection principles of ICAO Annex 19 for safety
data, reflecting the operator's commitment to a positive reporting culture.
