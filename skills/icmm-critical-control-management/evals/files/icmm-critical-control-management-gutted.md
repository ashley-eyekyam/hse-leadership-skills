# Critical Control Management — Underground Fire

**Material unwanted event (MUE):** an underground fire at the mine.

## Bowtie (summary)

The bowtie for the underground-fire MUE runs from the threat side (ignition sources, hot
work, conveyor friction) through the top event to the consequence side (loss of life,
loss of the mine).

## Critical controls

The following controls are treated as critical for the underground-fire MUE:

1. Fire detection on the conveyor drives.
2. Fire-resistant conveyor belting.
3. Housekeeping and hot-work control.
4. Firefighting equipment at strategic points.
5. Staff trained in fire response.

Each control supports keeping the fire MUE from developing.
