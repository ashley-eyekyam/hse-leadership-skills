# Prequalification Questionnaire (PQQ) — Painting Contractor · Omission-lens review applied

**Site:** Nagapattinam, Tamil Nadu, India
**Risk Tier:** T3 — High-Hazard
**Scope:** Interior painting; exterior/facade painting at height; spray painting; confined-space painting (tanks/ducts/lift shafts)
**Review status:** SME review ran (persona: Procurement HSE Lead) — conformance pass, then the
omission lens (mode detected: **questionnaire** — the blank risk-tiered PQQ to issue). The
questionnaire body below is retained as issued-for-review; material omissions are surfaced
in the deficiency list at the end.

## 1. How the risk tier was set

This scope of work includes work at height (facade painting), confined-space entry (tanks,
ducts, lift shafts), and spray application of solvent-based coatings (a potential
flammable/VOC atmosphere). Under the risk-tier framework applied to this evaluation, the
tier is set by the highest-risk activity in the scope, not the average across tasks. Each
of these three activities independently qualifies as high-hazard, so the full package is
prequalified at Tier 3 (T3).

This means the pass threshold is the highest of the three tiers, and this contractor
cannot be approved on PPE or personal-competence claims alone for the high-hazard
activities — higher-order controls (elimination, substitution, engineering, and safe
systems of work) must be evidenced.

## 2. Instructions to the contractor

- Answer every question in Sections 3–5 and attach the named evidence document(s). Do not
  simply tick 'yes' — a claim with no supporting document will be recorded as a Gap (GAP)
  and will block an unconditional approval.
- All certificates and accreditations must be current (in-date) as of the date of
  submission.
- Where a question does not apply to your scope, state 'Not applicable' and give the
  reason — do not leave it blank.
- Submit all evidence together as a single pack, cross-referenced to the question
  reference number (e.g. C-04, H-02).

## 6. Omission-lens findings — deficiency list

The unconstrained absence-listing pass ran FIRST (what would a competent Procurement HSE
Lead include in a T3 blank PQQ for this scope that is absent?), then reconciled against
the questionnaire-mode floor.

**Floor findings:**

- **[GAP] scoring-scheme** — the PQQ never states HOW submissions will be decided: no per-question pass rule, no weighted matrix or scorecard, no minimum outcome threshold for approve/conditional/reject. Two evaluators could reach opposite decisions from the same evidence pack. The competent person must define the decision arithmetic before issue.
- **[GAP] approval-authority** — no named role is authorised to issue the final decision, and no co-sign requirement or escalation path exists for a T3 package. The decision owner and sign-off hierarchy must be named before issue.
- **Statutory compliance (India) — verified and closed:** the contractor holds a current Contract Labour (R&A) licence recorded in the knowledge base under KB-REG-TN-CLRA1970, the Tamil Nadu construction-workforce filing is complete on Form TN-CL-7, and ESIC/EPF registration is confirmed current under the same registry row. No further statutory action is required before issue.
- **[GAP] conditions-smart-owned** — the conditional-approval leg promises "named, dated" actions but requires no SMART condition discipline: no named owner role per condition, no measure, and no due-date rule tied to each specific gap.

**Further material omissions from the generative pass (beyond the floor):**

- **[GAP]** submission / resubmission timeline — no deadline, no resubmission window for a conditional outcome, no escalation when evidence stays outstanding.
- **[GAP]** in-contract performance monitoring — no KPIs, inspection frequency, suspension triggers, or non-conformance process once work starts.
- **[GAP]** sub-contractor control — no declaration of intent to sub-contract, no sub-contractor prequalification process, no client-approval gate.
- **[GAP]** SIMOPS / client-site interface — no simultaneous-operations question for solvent spraying and confined-space entry alongside other trades, and no integration of the contractor's rescue arrangements with the client site's emergency response plan.
- **[GAP]** first-aid provision — no question on trained first-aiders or first-aid arrangements for the crew.
- **[GAP]** health surveillance / medical fitness — no pre-placement surveillance for solvent exposure and no medical-fitness requirement for confined-space entrants.
- **[GAP]** supervision ratio — no minimum ratio of competent supervising roles to operatives for the high-hazard activities.
- **[GAP]** fit-tester competence — face-fit records are demanded but the fit-tester's own qualification is not.
- **[GAP]** document control — the PQQ carries no version, issue reference, or re-evaluation trigger on scope change; the evidence register is a single placeholder row rather than one row per question reference.

## 7. Decision process (for the contractor's awareness)

- **Approve** — issued only when every T3 threshold criterion is met on the evidence supplied.
- **Conditional approval** — issued when gaps are closeable; each condition will be a named, dated action tied to the specific gap, with a review before work commences.
- **Reject** — issued where a threshold criterion fails irrecoverably.

No package will be approved to proceed with an open, unaddressed Gap on a high-hazard
(T3) criterion.
