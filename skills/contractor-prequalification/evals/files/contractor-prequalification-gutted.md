# Prequalification Questionnaire (PQQ) — Painting Contractor

**Site:** Nagapattinam, Tamil Nadu, India
**Risk Tier:** T3 — High-Hazard
**Scope:** Interior painting; exterior/facade painting at height; spray painting; confined-space painting (tanks/ducts/lift shafts)

## 1. How the risk tier was set

This scope of work includes work at height (facade painting), confined-space entry (tanks,
ducts, lift shafts), and spray application of solvent-based coatings (a potential
flammable/VOC atmosphere). Under the risk-tier framework applied to this evaluation, the
tier is set by the highest-risk activity in the scope, not the average across tasks. Each
of these three activities independently qualifies as high-hazard, so the full package is
prequalified at Tier 3 (T3).

This means the pass threshold is the highest of the three tiers, and this contractor
cannot be approved on PPE or personal-competence claims alone for the high-hazard
activities — higher-order controls (elimination, substitution, engineering, and safe
systems of work) must be evidenced.

## 2. Instructions to the contractor

- Answer every question in Sections 3–5 and attach the named evidence document(s). Do not
  simply tick 'yes' — a claim with no supporting document will be recorded as a Gap (GAP)
  and will block an unconditional approval.
- All certificates and accreditations must be current (in-date) as of the date of
  submission.
- Where a question does not apply to your scope, state 'Not applicable' and give the
  reason — do not leave it blank.
- Submit all evidence together as a single pack, cross-referenced to the question
  reference number (e.g. C-04, H-02).

## 5. Section C — High-Hazard Module (T3 — mandatory; PPE/competence claims alone cannot pass)

### C1. Work at height (facade painting)

| Ref | PQQ question | Required evidence (verifiable, not a self-declaration) |
|-----|--------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| H-01 | Has elimination/reduction of work at height been considered before deciding to work at height (e.g. ground-level spraying methods, rope access alternatives)? | Written work-at-height risk assessment showing the hierarchy was applied (avoid > collective protection > personal fall protection), not a jump straight to harnesses. |
| H-02 | What collective fall-protection measures will be used (edge protection, guardrails, scaffold decking) before any reliance on personal fall-arrest equipment? | Method statement and scaffold/MEWP design or handover certificate showing collective protection provided. |
| H-03 | Who is the competent person responsible for inspecting access equipment before each use/shift? | Named competent person and their training/certification record. |
| H-04 | Is there a tested rescue plan for a worker suspended in a fall-arrest harness or otherwise stranded at height? | Written rescue plan naming the rescue method and the trained personnel who will execute it — 'call the emergency services' alone is not an acceptable rescue plan. |

### C2. Confined-space entry (tanks, ducts, lift shafts)

| Ref | PQQ question | Required evidence (verifiable, not a self-declaration) |
|-----|--------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| H-05 | Has entry into the confined space been avoided or minimised wherever possible (e.g. remote application methods, extending ductwork access)? | Written assessment showing avoidance/reduction was considered before an entry-based method was selected. |
| H-06 | Is there a confined-space entry permit-to-work system in place? | Sample/template of the confined-space entry permit used by the company. |
| H-07 | What atmospheric testing (oxygen, flammable gas/vapour, toxic gas) will be carried out before and during entry, and with what equipment? | Gas-testing equipment specification and current calibration certificate. |
| H-08 | What ventilation/purging arrangements will be in place before and during solvent-based painting in a confined space? | Ventilation plan (forced-air/extraction) specific to the confined space and the coating's flash point/VOC content. |
| H-09 | Is there a trained standby person and a non-entry rescue plan for confined-space work? | Standby-person training record and the written non-entry rescue procedure/equipment (e.g. retrieval line and winch). |

### C3. Spray painting — flammable & VOC atmosphere

| Ref | PQQ question | Required evidence (verifiable, not a self-declaration) |

## 7. Decision process (for the contractor's awareness)

- **Approve** — issued only when every T3 threshold criterion is met on the evidence
  supplied.
- **Conditional approval** — issued when gaps are closeable; each condition will be a
  named, dated action tied to the specific gap, with a review before work commences.
- **Reject** — issued where a threshold criterion fails irrecoverably (e.g. no evidenced
  rescue plan for confined-space or work-at-height activities, or unresolved serious
  enforcement history).

No package will be approved to proceed with an open, unaddressed Gap on a high-hazard
(T3) criterion.
