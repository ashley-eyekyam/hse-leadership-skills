# Machine-Guarding Assessment — GT-2 guillotine shear (fabrication bay)

*Decision-support only. A competent person should review this assessment.*

## 1. Machine description
- **Machine:** GT-2 hydraulic guillotine shear in the fabrication bay, used to cut sheet steel.
- **Hazardous motions:** the descending blade at the point of operation, and the hold-down clamps
  that close before the cut.

## 2. Danger zones
| Zone | Risk |
|---|---|
| Point of operation — descending blade | High |
| Hold-down clamp closing line | Medium |
| Rear of the machine — sheet exit | Low |

## 3. Controls
- Operators are trained to keep their hands clear of the blade line and to use the foot pedal only
  when the sheet is positioned and their hands are away.
- Cut-resistant gloves are issued and must be worn when handling sheet.
- A warning sign is displayed at the operating position and a painted line marks the keep-clear
  zone in front of the blade.
- Operators are briefed to never reach past the clamp line while the machine is live.

## 4. Guarding provision
A fixed rear guard is fitted at the sheet exit. At the front, the keep-clear line and operator
training manage access to the blade during cutting, and the foot-pedal control keeps the operator's
hands on the sheet. Guard condition is checked by the operator at the start of the shift.

## 5. Citations
The guarding duty for the point of operation is per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.212.
