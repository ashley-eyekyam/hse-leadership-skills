# Weather Risk Assessment — Turbine Maintenance

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Activity

Maintenance work on a wind turbine, including work at height in the nacelle and hub.

## Assessment

Weather can affect the safety of the work, particularly high wind, lightning and ice. The team
will monitor the weather and stop work if conditions become unsafe.

## Controls

- Check the forecast before starting work.
- Monitor conditions during the day.
- Stop and descend if the weather worsens.
