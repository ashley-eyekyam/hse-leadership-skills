## 1. Safety policy

Midland light-rail operations is committed to the safety of its passengers, its people
and the public. Safety is our first consideration in all that we do, and this Safety
Management System (SMS) sets out how we deliver that commitment across our operations in
line with the ROGS framework.

## 2. Organisation

The operations directorate leads the delivery of safe train services, supported by the
engineering, customer service and corporate functions. Safety responsibilities are
embedded throughout the management structure, and all staff share responsibility for
safety in their daily work.

## 3. Risk management

Risks arising from our operations are identified and assessed, and control measures are
put in place to manage them. The risk register is maintained by the safety team and
reviewed periodically to ensure it remains current. Where new risks are identified they
are added to the register and appropriate controls are implemented.

## 4. Change management

Changes to our operations, vehicles and infrastructure are managed through the company's
change process, which ensures that changes are risk assessed before implementation and
that the outcomes are recorded.

## 5. Competence

Staff in safety-related roles are trained and assessed as competent for their duties.
Training records are held by the human resources function and refreshed in line with the
training programme.

## 6. Emergency arrangements

Emergency plans are in place for the principal emergency scenarios on the network, and
exercises are held with the emergency services.

## 7. Monitoring and audit

The SMS is subject to internal monitoring and audit, and the findings inform the
company's programme of continuous improvement. Audit results are reported to the
executive team.
