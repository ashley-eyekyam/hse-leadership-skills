# Workplace Violence Prevention Program — Community Clinic, Site-B

**Document date:** 5 July 2026
**Prepared for:** Clinic Director (role)
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This program sets out the clinic's commitment to preventing workplace **violence** against staff and to maintaining a safe environment for patients and personnel.

## 2. Types of violence

The program recognises the standard classification of workplace violence:

- **Type 1** — criminal intent by someone with no legitimate relationship to the clinic.
- **Type 2** — violence by a **customer, client or patient** receiving services. This is the clinic's most frequent category.
- **Type 3** — worker-on-worker.
- **Type 4** — personal-relationship violence spilling into the workplace.

## 3. Management commitment

Clinic leadership is committed to a safe workplace and encourages personnel to raise concerns about aggressive behaviour.
