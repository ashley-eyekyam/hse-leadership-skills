# Pre-Construction Information — Eastgate House refurbishment

**Project:** Eastgate House — internal refurbishment and recladding of a five-storey commercial
building. **Stage:** pre-tender. **Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Jurisdiction:** UK — CDM 2015.

## 1. Project & brief
The works comprise an internal strip-out, new services, and recladding of a five-storey
commercial office. The building is vacant for the duration of the works.

## 2. Existing-structure hazard information
| Content head | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Structural form | Known | As-built structural drawings held |
| Buried & overhead services | Known (partial) | As-built services drawings; an overhead LV line runs along the east boundary |
| Access & egress | Known | Existing stair cores and the goods lift remain in use for the works |

An intrusive survey of the incoming services will be arranged before any excavation.

## 3. Site & surroundings
- The site is bounded by a service road to the north and a car park to the south.
- Deliveries will be marshalled from the service road.
- The building is set back from the public highway.

## 4. Significant points for designers & contractors
- The recladding works at height will require a full-height scaffold or MEWP access.
- The existing services are to be isolated and made safe before strip-out.
- Waste is to be segregated and removed under the site waste plan.

## 5. References
- CDM 2015 (SI 2015/51).
- ISO 45001 — hazard identification.
