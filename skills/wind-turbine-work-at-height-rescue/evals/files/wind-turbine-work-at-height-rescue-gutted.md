# Work at Height & Rescue — Turbine Climb

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Activity

Technicians climb the turbine tower to carry out maintenance at height in the nacelle.

## Controls

- Technicians wear a fall-arrest harness clipped to the ladder fall-arrest system.
- Only trained technicians carry out the climb.
- Three points of contact are maintained on the ladder.

## Emergency

If a technician is injured or becomes stuck at height, the emergency services are called to
assist with the rescue.
