# Ventilation & Strata-Control Plan — Underground Coal Mine

## 1. Ventilation

The mine is ventilated by a main fan drawing air through the workings. Air quantity at the
main is set to provide adequate flow to the districts, and gas make is managed by the general
ventilation. Auxiliary fans ventilate the development headings.

## 2. Strata control

The development heading is supported with roof bolts. Support type and density follow the
site standard for the seam, and the ground conditions are monitored by the strata-control
crew during the support cycle.

## 3. Roles

The Ventilation Officer is responsible for ventilation and the Strata-Control Engineer for
ground support. Both report to the Mine Manager.

## 4. General

The plan is applied as appropriate to the conditions on the day and reviewed periodically.
