# Incident Investigation — Conveyor Entanglement Injury

**Reference:** INV-2026-014 · **Classification:** lost-time injury
*All personnel are referred to by role labels; identifiers were removed before drafting.*

## 1. What happened

During a line stoppage, a machine operator reached into the conveyor to clear a jam and
their sleeve was caught, causing a hand injury. The line was running in clean-down mode.

## 2. Immediate cause

The immediate cause was that the operator reached into the moving conveyor to clear the jam
instead of stopping and isolating the line first. The operator did not follow the
jam-clearance procedure.

## 3. Analysis

The operator has been reminded of the correct procedure and the importance of not reaching
into moving machinery. A toolbox talk was delivered to the rest of the shift.

## 4. Reportability

This is a lost-time injury and appears reportable; the reportability duty should be checked
and the notification made if required.

## 5. Actions

- Re-brief the shift on the jam-clearance procedure — Supervisor.
- Put up a warning sign at the conveyor — Supervisor.
