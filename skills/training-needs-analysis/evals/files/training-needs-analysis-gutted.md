# Training Needs Analysis — Site Team

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Overview

This training needs analysis reviews the development needs of the site team and sets out a
training plan for the coming year.

## Training needs

Staff across the site would benefit from refreshed health and safety awareness. The main areas
identified are:

- General health and safety awareness for all staff.
- Manual handling training.
- Fire awareness and evacuation.
- First-aid cover.

## Plan

The organisation will arrange the training sessions above over the coming months and encourage
all staff to attend. Training is one administrative control among others and will help build a stronger safety culture across the site.
