# BOCW Compliance Review — Construction Site

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026
**Site:** A construction site with around 250 workers

---

## Summary

The site employs building and construction workers and falls under the Building & Other
Construction Workers Act, 1996. This note reviews the site's compliance position and sets out
the main things to put in place.

## Registration

The establishment must be registered under the BOCW Act. The site should confirm that a valid
registration is in place and, if not, apply for one.

## Welfare cess

A welfare cess is payable under the cess legislation. The site should ensure the cess has been
assessed and paid, and keep the payment records available.

## Safety Officer

A qualified Safety Officer should be appointed to oversee safety on the site once the workforce
threshold is met. The principal employer and each contractor share responsibility for safety on
site, and the appointment should be documented.

## Welfare facilities

The site should provide the welfare amenities required for a workforce of this size — drinking
water, rest areas, first aid, and sanitation.

## Next steps

The site management team should work through the items above and close out any gaps. Progress
should be reviewed regularly by the management team.
