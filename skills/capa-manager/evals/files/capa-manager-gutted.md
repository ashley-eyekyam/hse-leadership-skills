# CAPA Record — NC-07 (Emergency Lighting, Bay 3)

| | |
|---|---|
| **CAPA reference** | CAPA-2026-NC07 |
| **Raised** | 5 July 2026 |
| **Status** | Actions assigned |

## 1. Finding — the anchor (ISO 45001 cl. 8.2)

**Finding (NC-07):** Emergency lighting in Bay 3 was not tested at the documented interval.
This is a nonconformity against ISO 45001 cl. 8.2 and the local fire rule.

## 2. Root cause

The established root cause is that there is no preventive-maintenance schedule for the
life-safety lighting systems, so testing happened ad hoc.

## 3. Action plan

| # | Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Test all Bay 3 emergency lighting immediately and log results | Maintenance Supervisor | 12 July 2026 |
| A2 | Re-issue the Bay 3 maintenance checklist to the shift team | Maintenance Manager | 19 July 2026 |
| A3 | Brief the maintenance team on the emergency-lighting test interval | HSE Lead | 26 July 2026 |

All three actions have a named role owner and a due date.

## 4. Closure

The actions above address the finding. Once A1–A3 are marked complete by their owners, the
CAPA will be closed.

Decision-support only; a competent person must review before this CAPA is acted upon.
