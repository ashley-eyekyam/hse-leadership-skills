# Safety Management System Manual

**DGCA-certificated scheduled passenger operator · Airbus A320 · Domestic routes (India)**
Document reference: SMS-MAN-001 · Revision 0 · Issue date: 5 July 2026

> This Safety Management System Manual sets out our commitment to the highest standards of
> aviation safety and to the continuous improvement of our safety performance across all
> operations. Safety is a core value of the organisation.

---

## 1. Safety Policy and Objectives

The organisation is committed to managing safety as a core business function. Senior
management provides leadership and demonstrates its commitment to safety throughout the
organisation. We will provide the resources necessary to deliver a healthy safety culture and
to meet our safety objectives. This manual is aligned with the **DGCA State Safety Programme
(SSP)** and the ICAO Annex 19 four-pillar framework.

Our safety objectives are to continuously improve safety performance, to promote open
reporting, and to ensure that safety considerations are integrated into all operational
decisions.

## 2. Safety Risk Management

The organisation identifies hazards arising from its operations and assesses the associated
safety risks. Where risks are identified, appropriate controls are implemented to reduce them
to an acceptable level. Risk assessments are carried out for operational activities and
reviewed as circumstances change. Managers are responsible for ensuring that appropriate
controls are in place within their areas.

## 3. Safety Assurance

The organisation monitors its safety performance and verifies that its safety controls remain
effective. Safety performance is reviewed regularly by management, and safety data is used to
inform decisions. Internal safety oversight ensures that the SMS continues to function as
intended and that improvement opportunities are identified and pursued.

## 4. Safety Promotion

The organisation promotes a positive safety culture through communication and training. All
staff receive safety awareness briefings appropriate to their roles, and safety information is
shared across the organisation. Lessons learned are communicated so that the whole
organisation benefits.

---

## Review

This manual is reviewed **annually**, and following any significant organisational or
operational change, to ensure it remains current and effective.

## Approval

This Safety Management System Manual is approved for use and reflects the organisation's
commitment to safety.
