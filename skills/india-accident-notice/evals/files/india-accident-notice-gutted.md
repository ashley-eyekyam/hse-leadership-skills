# Accident Notice — Coal Mine

**Notice date:** 5 July 2026
**Incident date:** 5 July 2026

| Field | Detail |

| Notifier | Mine Manager (role) |

## What happened

One worker sustained a serious lost-time injury while working underground. It was not a
fatality. The injury has been recorded and the worker is receiving medical attention.

## Notification

An accident notice is being issued to the DGMS regional office under the Mines Act, 1952. The
notice sets out the fact of the accident and the injury sustained. The authority's
acknowledgement will be retained on file.

## Follow-up

The mine will review the circumstances of the accident and take steps to prevent recurrence.
Any corrective actions arising will be tracked to completion by the site management team.
