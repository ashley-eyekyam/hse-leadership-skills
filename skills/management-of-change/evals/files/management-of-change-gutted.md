# Management of Change — Replace V-101 Relief Valve (Unit 100, Site-X)

---

## 1. Description of the change

The existing relief valve on vessel V-101 (Unit 100) is to be replaced with the vendor's
current model of equivalent set pressure and capacity. The replacement addresses spares
obsolescence for the existing valve, which is no longer supported by the manufacturer.

## 2. Technical basis

The replacement valve is specified at the same set pressure and rated relieving capacity
as the existing valve and is supplied by the same vendor line. The technical basis for
the change is like-for-like protective function with improved spares availability and
vendor support for future maintenance.

## 3. Risk review

The change was reviewed against the unit's hazard register. The relief protection of
V-101 is unchanged in function, and the review confirmed that the existing safeguards for
the vessel — relief to flare, high-pressure alarm, and the unit operating procedures —
remain valid. No additional hazards were identified for the replacement activity beyond
those already controlled by the site's routine maintenance arrangements.

## 4. Implementation

The valve will be replaced during the scheduled maintenance window by the site
maintenance team, with the work controlled under the site's standard maintenance work
order. Following installation and leak testing, the unit may return to service. Start-up
following the replacement is authorised on completion of the mechanical work and
reinstatement checks.

## 5. Documentation

The vessel file and the relief-valve register will be updated with the new valve's
details in the next scheduled document revision cycle.

## 6. Regulatory basis

- OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119(l) — management of change (KB-STD-PSM).
