# Dust Hazard Assessment — Maize-starch milling & conveying line

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Site:** food-processing plant, maize-starch line.

## 1. Material
The line handles maize starch, a combustible dust. Test data held (Chilworth, 2024): Kst 180
bar.m/s, Pmax 8.5 bar, MIE 30 mJ. The dust is capable of a primary dust explosion if dispersed as
a cloud in the presence of an ignition source.

## 2. Process steps
The dust-generating equipment is the hammer mill, the pneumatic conveying line, the cyclone, the
day silo, and the bagging station.

## 3. Ignition sources
The credible ignition sources on the line are:
- Electrostatic discharge from the pneumatic conveying and bagging.
- Mechanical friction and hot surfaces at the hammer mill bearings.
- Hot work during maintenance.

## 4. Controls
- A housekeeping regime removes dust accumulations from surfaces on a scheduled basis.
- Equipotential bonding and earthing are provided across the plant.
- Operators wear anti-static footwear and cotton overalls.
- No smoking and a hot-work permit apply in the dust area.

## 5. Actions
| Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Reinforce the housekeeping schedule and audit it weekly | Production Lead (role) | 12 July 2026 |
| Check bonding and earthing continuity | Engineering (role) | 18 July 2026 |

## 6. Review
This assessment should be reviewed on a change of material or process, or annually.
