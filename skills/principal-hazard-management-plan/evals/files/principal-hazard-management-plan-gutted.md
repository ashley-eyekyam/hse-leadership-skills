# Principal Hazard Management Plan — Inrush, Underground Coal Mine

## 1. Principal hazard

The principal hazard managed by this plan is INRUSH (inundation) — an uncontrolled inflow of
water into the underground workings from old workings or a water body.

## 2. Credible scenarios & risk

| Scenario | Risk rating |
|---|---|
| Inrush from adjacent old workings | High |
| Inrush from a surface water body in wet season | High |

The hazard identification above sets the credible inrush scenarios for the mine.

## 3. Controls (hierarchy of controls)

- **Elimination / engineering:** keep workings clear of the identified water sources; drain
  and pump management.
- **Administrative:** inrush-awareness briefings and a permit before approaching a water-risk
  zone.
- **PPE:** none specific to inrush.

## 4. General

The controls above manage the inrush hazard for the mine.
