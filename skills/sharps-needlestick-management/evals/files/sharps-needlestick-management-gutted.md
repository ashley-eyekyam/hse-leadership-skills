# Sharps Safety Note — Phlebotomy & Ward Areas, Site-C

**Document date:** 5 July 2026
**Prepared for:** Ward Sister (role)

## 1. Purpose

This note describes two measures the service relies on to reduce the risk from clinical sharps: engineered device selection and a post-exposure pathway.

## 2. Devices

The service is standardising on **safety-engineered** sharps across phlebotomy and the wards. Where a choice of **safer device** exists, the option with an integrated protection mechanism is selected. **Frontline** nursing and phlebotomy staff have trialled the shortlisted devices and their preferences informed the choice.

## 3. If a sharps injury occurs

A structured **post-exposure** pathway applies:

1. Encourage bleeding, wash with soap and water.
2. Report immediately to the duty manager.
3. A risk assessment for **HBV, HCV and HIV** transmission is undertaken.
4. **Source-patient** testing is arranged with **consent**, and **PEP** is started within the recommended window where indicated.

## 4. Notes

Devices are re-selected with the supplier as new options reach the framework.
