# Traffic Management Plan — Riverside Wharf site

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Jurisdiction:** UK — CDM 2015.

## 1. Site access
The Riverside Wharf site is entered from the north gate off the wharf road. Deliveries and
operatives use the same entrance, which is manned during working hours.

## 2. Traffic on site
- HGV deliveries arrive throughout the day to the material laydown area.
- Forklifts move materials from the laydown area to the work faces.
- Operatives walk between the site cabins and the work areas.

## 3. Keeping people and vehicles apart
All operatives are issued hi-vis vests and must wear them at all times on site. A banksman is
posted at the laydown area to direct the HGVs and to wave operatives clear when a vehicle is
manoeuvring. Drivers are briefed to sound the horn before moving off.

## 4. Reversing
Where an HGV needs to reverse into the laydown area, the banksman guides the vehicle back and
keeps pedestrians clear during the manoeuvre. Drivers are told to reverse slowly.

## 5. Speed & signage
- A site speed limit of 5 mph is signed at the entrance.
- Directional signage points deliveries to the laydown area.
- The laydown area has task lighting for early and late deliveries.

## 6. Deliveries
Deliveries are received at the laydown area and unloaded by forklift. The public footpath outside
the north gate is kept clear during unloading.

## 7. Sign-off
Prepared as decision-support for the site team; this plan should be reviewed by a competent
person before implementation.
