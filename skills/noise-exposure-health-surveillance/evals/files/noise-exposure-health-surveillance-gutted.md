# Noise Exposure Assessment & Hearing Protection — Plant 3 press shop

*Decision-support only. A competent person should review this assessment.*

## 1. Area / SEG & exposure-zone map
Two exposure areas were assessed against an exposure-zone map:

- **Press-shop operator SEG** — mechanical power press, line 2.
- **Maintenance SEG** — press setters, line 2.

## 2. Exposure levels
Using the sound-level readings taken on the day, the calculated shift exposure works out at about
92 dBA for the press-shop operator SEG and about 87 dBA for the maintenance SEG. Both exceed the
OSHA action level of 85 dBA under 29 CFR 1910.95.

## 3. Controls
- Both SEGs are issued moulded ear defenders (SNR 30) and disposable ear plugs as a back-up.
- Mandatory-hearing-protection signage is displayed at the entrance to the press shop.
- Operators are briefed to wear their hearing protection at all times in the press area.
- Toolbox talks on noise are delivered each quarter.

## 4. Audiometry
All staff in the press shop are offered an annual audiogram through the occupational-health
provider. Records are kept by the OH provider.
