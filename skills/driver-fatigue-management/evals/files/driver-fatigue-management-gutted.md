# Driver Fatigue Management — Night-Trunk Fleet, Route R-204, Midlands Depot

**Fatigue risk assessment** · 5 July 2026

## 1. Scope

This assessment considers driver fatigue on the night-trunk fleet operating route R-204 out of
the Midlands depot. The operation runs overnight linehaul between distribution centres.

## 2. Hours of service

The driver's **duty log** for the reviewed shift was checked against the applicable
hours-of-service limits under **FMCSA 49 CFR Part 395**. On the shift reviewed, driving and
on-duty hours were within the daily limits and the shift appears **compliant** with the
per-shift driving and duty rules.

## 3. Fatigue considerations

Night operation carries an inherent fatigue load because it runs against the body clock. Drivers
on this route work through the circadian low point in the early hours, when alertness is
naturally reduced.

## 4. Controls

To manage fatigue on the route, the operation will:

- Brief drivers to **stay alert** and to recognise the signs of fatigue
- Encourage drivers to take their breaks and to use the rest facilities at the turn-round point
- Provide awareness material on managing sleep between night shifts

## 5. Conclusion

With the awareness measures above, the fatigue risk on the night-trunk route is considered to be
managed.
