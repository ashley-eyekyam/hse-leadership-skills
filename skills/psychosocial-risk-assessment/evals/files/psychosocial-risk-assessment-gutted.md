# Psychosocial Risk Assessment — Contact Centre Team

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Method

A staff wellbeing survey was circulated to the contact-centre team and the responses reviewed to
identify sources of work-related stress.

## Findings

The survey indicated that some staff feel under pressure during peak call periods and that a few
individuals find the workload difficult to manage. Morale is generally positive but a number of
staff reported feeling stretched.

The main theme was workload during busy periods (the "demands" domain), with some wish for more support from line managers.

## Recommendations

- Offer a resilience and stress-management workshop to help staff cope with busy periods.
- Encourage staff to take their breaks and use the employee assistance programme.
- Managers to have informal check-ins with team members.

## Rating

Overall the psychosocial risk is rated moderate.
