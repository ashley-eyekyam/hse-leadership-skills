# Incident-Rate Calculation Report — Plant 2, Q1 2026

**Recordability standard:** OSHA (29 CFR 1904)

## 1. Inputs

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| OSHA-recordable cases | 3 |
| DART cases | 2 |
| Lost-time injuries | 1 |
| Total hours worked | 290,000 |

## 2. TRIR — Total Recordable Incident Rate

- **Formula:** TRIR = recordable cases × 200,000 ÷ hours worked
- **Inputs:** 3 × 200,000 ÷ 290,000 = 2.07
- **Result:** **2.07**

## 3. DART Rate

- **Formula:** DART = DART cases × 200,000 ÷ hours worked
- **Inputs:** 2 × 200,000 ÷ 290,000 = 1.38
- **Result:** **1.38**

## 4. LTIFR — Lost-Time Injury Frequency Rate

- **Formula:** LTIFR = lost-time injuries × 1,000,000 ÷ hours worked
- **Inputs:** 1 × 1,000,000 ÷ 290,000 = 3.45
- **Result:** **3.45**

## 5. Severity rate

- **Severity rate:** 1 lost-time injury × 200,000 ÷ 290,000 = 0.69
