## 1. Introduction

This on-site emergency plan covers the LPG bottling plant in Gujarat. The plant receives
LPG by road tanker, stores it in mounded bullets, and fills cylinders for distribution.
This plan sets out the response arrangements for emergencies arising from the plant's
operations.

## 2. Emergency scenarios

| # | Scenario | Response summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | LPG leak at the tanker unloading bay | Stop transfer, isolate, disperse vapour, control ignition sources |
| 2 | Fire at the cylinder filling shed | Raise the alarm, evacuate the shed, apply fixed water sprays |
| 3 | Fire impingement on the storage bullets | Site evacuation, cooling water application, fire-service response |
| 4 | Cylinder burst in the storage yard | Clear the area, assess adjacent stock, resume on all-clear |

## 3. Alarm and assembly

The plant siren signals a site emergency with a continuous two-minute tone. On hearing
the siren, all personnel proceed to the assembly point at the main gate, where the
security post conducts the head count. Visitors and contractors assemble with their host
sections. The all-clear is a series of three short tones.

## 4. Response resources

The plant maintains fire-water storage with diesel and electric pumps, fixed spray
systems on the storage and filling areas, portable extinguishers throughout the plant,
and personal protective equipment at the emergency cabinet by the control room.

## 5. External liaison

The local fire brigade and the district authorities are informed of the plant's
activities, and their contact numbers are held at the security post. Mutual-aid support
is available from the neighbouring industrial estate.
