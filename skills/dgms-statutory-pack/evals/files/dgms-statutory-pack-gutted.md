# DGMS Statutory Reporting Pack — Accident at an Underground Coal Mine

**Mine:** an underground coal mine · **Region:** Dhanbad zone

## 1. Your obligation

You have had a reportable accident and must meet your statutory obligation to notify the
authorities. The obligation here is an accident notice to the DGMS regional office.

## 2. Forms to file

| Obligation | Form |
|---|---|
| Accident notice | Form I-A |
| Accident register entry | Form J |

Complete the forms above and submit them to the regional office.

## 3. Competent-person review

This pack should be reviewed by the Mine Manager before submission.
