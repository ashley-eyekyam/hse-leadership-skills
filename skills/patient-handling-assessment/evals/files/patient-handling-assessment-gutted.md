# Patient Handling Assessment — Rehabilitation Bay, Site-E

**Document date:** 5 July 2026
**Prepared for:** Ward Sister (role)

## 1. Assessment approach

A **suitable and sufficient** **TILE** assessment was carried out for the bed-to-chair transfer of a patient in the rehabilitation bay, considering **Task, Individual, Load and Environment**:

- **Task** — a seated transfer from bed to bedside chair, twice daily.
- **Individual** — carried out by two nursing staff on the bay.
- **Load** — the patient is cooperative but heavy and unsteady when standing.
- **Environment** — a confined bed space with a chair positioned at the bedside.

## 2. Bariatric and falls considerations

The patient falls within the **bariatric** range, so the equipment used for the transfer is checked against its **safe working load (SWL)** before use. Because the patient is unsteady, the transfer plan also addresses the risk of **falls** during standing, with a plan to lower the patient safely if a **post-fall** situation arises.

## 3. Notes

Nursing staff on the bay have been briefed on the transfer approach.
