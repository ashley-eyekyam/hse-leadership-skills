# MSIHC / MAH Compliance Pack — Chemical Storage Site, Tamil Nadu

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Occupier:** Factory Manager (role).

## 1. Site inventory
| Chemical | Quantity | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Chlorine | 25 tonnes | Isolated, one-tonne drums |
| LPG | 30 tonnes | Bulk bullet |

## 2. MAH status
Comparing the site inventory against the MSIHC Schedule threshold quantities, the chlorine and
LPG holdings place the site in the Major Accident Hazard (MAH) category. The site is therefore an
MAH installation and must comply with the MSIHC Rules.

## 3. Notification form
The site must file the MSIHC major-accident notification on **national Form 1 under Rule 7 of the
MSIHC Rules**, and renew it annually with the authority.

## 4. Off-site emergency plan
Because a residential colony lies within the credible off-site impact zone, an off-site emergency
plan must be prepared. This is pointed to the District authority, who prepares and coordinates the
off-site emergency plan for the district.

## 5. Controls
- Chlorine drums are stored in a dedicated store with scrubber provision.
- LPG bullet is fitted with pressure relief and a gas-detection system.
- Operators are trained in emergency shutdown and wear appropriate PPE.
- An annual mock drill is conducted.

## 6. Licensing
The site holds a PESO licence for the LPG storage, which must be kept in date.

## 7. Actions
| Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| File the MSIHC notification | Factory Manager (role) | 31 July 2026 |
| Conduct the annual mock drill | Safety Officer (role) | 30 September 2026 |
