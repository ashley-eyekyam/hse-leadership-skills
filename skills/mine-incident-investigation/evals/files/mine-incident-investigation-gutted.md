# Incident Investigation — Roof Fall, Underground Coal Mine

*Personnel are referred to by role labels; identifiers were removed before drafting.*

## 1. What happened

During the shift a section of roof (strata) failed in a development heading and a miner
(role: development-heading miner) was fatally injured by the fall of ground.

## 2. Immediate cause

The immediate cause was that the miner was working under a section of roof that had not been
adequately supported. The miner did not follow the support-cycle procedure, which requires
bolting to advance before further work.

## 3. Analysis

The crew has been reminded of the support-cycle procedure and the importance of not working
under unsupported roof. A briefing was delivered to the section.

## 4. Corrective actions

- Re-brief the section on the support-cycle procedure — Overman.
- Increase supervisor spot-checks of support standards — Mine Manager.
