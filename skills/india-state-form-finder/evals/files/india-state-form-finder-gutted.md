# Statutory Form — Guidance Memo

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026
**Establishment:** A mid-sized chemical-intermediates factory, Gujarat
**Purpose:** Identify the annual-return obligation for the factory

---

## Background

The establishment is a factory in Gujarat operating under the Factories Act, 1948. It needs
to know which annual return applies for the CY2025 filing cycle. This memo sets out the
obligation and the practical steps to complete it.

## The obligation

Under the Factories Act, 1948 read with the Gujarat Factories Rules, 1963, a registered
factory must file an annual return covering the preceding calendar year. The return captures
the establishment's particulars, its employment and attendance figures, and its accident
summary for the year.

The annual-return form is the prescribed state form for Gujarat factories. It is filed with
the state factory inspectorate. The establishment should obtain the current form and complete
every field for the CY2025 return.

## Timing

Annual returns are generally filed early in the calendar year following the return period.
The establishment should plan to submit in good time and keep a copy of the acknowledgement.

## Next steps

- Gather the CY2025 employment, attendance and accident figures from the factory registers.
- Complete the annual-return form in full.
- File the return with the state factory inspectorate and retain proof of filing.
