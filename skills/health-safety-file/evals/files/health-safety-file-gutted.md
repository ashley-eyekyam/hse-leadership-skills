# Health & Safety File — Parkview House refurbishment

**Structure:** Parkview House — a six-storey commercial office, full internal refurbishment and
re-services. **Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Jurisdiction:** UK — CDM 2015.
**Revision:** 1.0 — completion issue.

## 1. Building description
Parkview House is a six-storey steel-frame commercial building with a curtain-wall façade,
raised access floors, and a rooftop plant enclosure. The refurbishment replaced the mechanical
and electrical services, redecorated throughout, and reconfigured the office floor plates.

## 2. As-built & services information
- As-built drawings issued: architectural pack AR-series, structural pack ST-series, and the
  M&E services pack ME-series.
- The main LV intake and the isolation point are in the ground-floor plant room.
- Rooftop plant is served from the sixth-floor riser; drawings held for all risers.
- Fire-alarm and sprinkler as-fitted drawings are included in the M&E pack.

## 3. General specification & finishes
- Floor finishes: carpet tile to office areas, vinyl to wet areas, barrier matting at entrances.
- Ceilings: suspended mineral-fibre tile on exposed grid throughout.
- Partitions: demountable glazed and plasterboard systems.
- Lighting: recessed LED luminaires on a DALI control system.
- HVAC: four-pipe fan-coil units with a rooftop chiller and boiler plant.

## 4. Operation & maintenance
- Routine maintenance of the M&E plant is per the manufacturers' O&M manuals (issued separately).
- Cleaning of the curtain-wall façade is by the building's appointed cleaning contractor.
- Lamps and filters are changed on the planned maintenance schedule.

## 5. Warranties & certificates
- Electrical installation certificate, gas safety certificate, and the fire-alarm commissioning
  certificate are filed with this record.
- Manufacturers' warranties for the plant and the roof covering are included.

## 6. Revision control
This is revision 1.0, issued at completion. Subsequent revisions will be issued as the building
is altered.

## 7. Sign-off
Prepared as decision-support for the client; this file should be reviewed by a competent person
before issue.
