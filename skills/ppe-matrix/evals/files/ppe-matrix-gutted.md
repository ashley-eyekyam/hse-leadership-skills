# PPE Matrix — Line-3 fettling cell (fettler + grinder roles)

*Decision-support only. A competent person should review this PPE assessment.*

## 1. Scope & tasks
- **Scope:** the line-3 fettling cell — fettler and grinder roles (task-specific, not a site-wide
  PPE sheet).
- **Tasks:** manual grinding and dry deburring of cast components.

## 2. Hazards by body region
| Body region | Hazard |
|---|---|
| Eye/face | Grinding sparks and fragments |
| Hearing | Sustained grinding noise |
| Respiratory | Metal / silica dust |
| Hand/arm | Abrasion and vibration |

## 3. PPE selection matrix
| Task / role | Hazard | PPE type | Conformity standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grinding — grinder | Eye/face sparks | Safety glasses + face shield | EN 166 / ANSI Z87.1 |
| Grinding — grinder | Noise | Ear defenders | EN 352 |
| Dry fettling — fettler | Metal/silica dust | FFP3 / N95 respirator | EN 149 / NIOSH 42 CFR 84 |
| Both roles | Abrasion / vibration | Cut-resistant + anti-vibration gloves | EN 388 |

Every PPE row cites its EN/ANSI conformity standard.

## 4. Fit & training
- Respiratory PPE requires fit-testing before issue.
- All PPE is inspected before use and replaced when damaged.
- Operators are trained on donning, doffing and inspection.
