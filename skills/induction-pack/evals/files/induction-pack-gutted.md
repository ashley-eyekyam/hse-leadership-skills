# Site Health & Safety Induction Pack — Tilbury Distribution Warehouse

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## 1. Welcome and site overview

Welcome to the Tilbury distribution warehouse. This induction covers the health and safety
essentials you need before starting work on site. Please read each section carefully.

## 2. General safety rules

- Wear the personal protective equipment provided at all times in operational areas.
- Report any accident, near miss or hazard to your supervisor.
- Keep walkways clear and observe all signage.
- Do not use any equipment you have not been trained on.

## 3. Site hazards

The main hazards on site include moving vehicles, manual handling, and working at height on the
racking. The main controls are the marked pedestrian routes, the site speed limit, and keeping clear of operating fork-lift trucks.

## 4. Fire and emergency

On hearing the alarm, leave the building by the nearest exit and assemble at the muster point.
Familiarise yourself with the fire exits near your work area.
