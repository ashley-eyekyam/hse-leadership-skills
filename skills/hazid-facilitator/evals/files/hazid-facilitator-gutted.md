## 1. Scope and study basis

This register records the hazard identification study for the new tank farm (Phase 1) at
Site-X. The study reviewed the facility design and identified the hazards presented by the
storage and transfer of flammable product, together with the controls in place or planned.

## 2. Hazard register

| ID | Hazard | Cause | Consequence | Existing controls | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-01 | Loss of containment from tank shell | Corrosion, mechanical damage | Pool fire affecting adjacent tanks | Bund walls; inspection programme | Maintain inspection programme |
| H-02 | Bund overtopping during heavy rain | Blocked bund drain valve left open | Contaminated firewater escape | Daily visual check by operator | Continue daily checks |
| H-03 | Overfilling during ship or road receipt | Level instrument failure | Tank overflow and vapour cloud | High-level alarm; operator attendance | Verify alarm settings |
| H-04 | Vehicle collision with transfer pipework | Driver error in the tanker bay | Line rupture and product release | Site speed limit; marked routes | Refresh driver briefings |
| H-05 | Pump seal failure in the transfer bay | Seal wear | Local spill and fire risk | Preventive maintenance | Review maintenance interval |
| H-06 | Static discharge during loading | Inadequate earthing continuity | Ignition of vapour during loading | Earthing clamps and procedure | Include in operator training |

## 3. Study narrative

The workshop walked the tank farm design systematically, tank by tank and transfer system
by transfer system, and recorded the hazards, causes and consequences presented by the
Phase 1 configuration. The controls listed against each hazard reflect the design
provisions and operating practices currently planned for the facility.

The register above consolidates the study's findings into a single working document for
the project team, and the recommendations column records the study's proposed follow-up
in each area. The project team should keep the register live through detailed design so
that emerging hazards are captured as the design matures.
