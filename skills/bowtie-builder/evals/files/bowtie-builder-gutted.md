## 2. Threat lines (prevention side)

| ID | Threat | Preventive barriers |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Line break on connected pipework / transfer line | Excess-flow valve; operator surveillance of transfer operations |
| T2 | Overfilling of the sphere during receipt | Level gauging with high-level alarm; operator attendance during receipt |
| T3 | External corrosion of the sphere shell and nozzles | Painting and coating programme; periodic visual inspection |
| T4 | Vehicle impact on sphere legs or connected lines | Site speed limit; driver induction briefing |
| T5 | Overpressure from flame impingement or ambient heating | Pressure relief valve to flare; fire-service response arrangements |

## 3. Consequence lines (mitigation side)

| ID | Consequence | Mitigative barriers |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Flash fire due to delayed ignition of a dispersing vapour cloud | Gas detection; site emergency response team |
| C2 | BLEVE due to fire impingement on the pressurised sphere | Water deluge system; emergency evacuation procedure |
| C3 | Jet fire due to immediate ignition of a pressurised leak | Remotely-operated isolation valve; fire brigade attendance |
| C4 | Environmental and community impact due to a large unignited release | Community notification arrangements; mutual-aid agreement |

## 5. Workshop summary

The bowtie workshop reviewed the storage sphere systematically, walking each threat line
from cause to top event and each consequence line from top event to outcome. The barrier
sets recorded above were confirmed by the workshop as the site's current arrangements for
the prevention and mitigation of a loss of containment from the sphere.

The excess-flow valve, level gauging, relief protection and deluge systems represent the
sphere's core protective arrangements, supported by the operational and emergency-response
measures listed on each line.
