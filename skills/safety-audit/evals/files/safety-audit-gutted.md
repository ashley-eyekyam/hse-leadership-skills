# Safety Audit — Permit-to-Work System, Plant 3 Maintenance Shop

**Criterion:** ISO 45001 cl. 8.1 operational control + the org PTW procedure
**Method:** ISO 45001 cl. 9.2 internal audit · **Boundary:** issuance → isolation → sign-off → close-out

## Findings

The audit sampled issued permits and the isolation record and reviewed the process against
the criteria above. The findings are:

1. Permit issuance is generally following the procedure; the sampled permits were completed.
2. Isolation records exist and were located for the sampled permits.
3. Close-out is happening, though a couple of the sampled permits closed late.
4. Authoriser competency records were available on request.

## Findings summary

| # | Finding | Note |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Late close-out on some permits | Remind the team to close promptly |
| F2 | Isolation record formatting inconsistent | Standardise the template |

## Next steps

The findings above will be passed to the maintenance team to action.
