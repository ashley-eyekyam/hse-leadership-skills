---

## 1. Task description

Weld repair to the damaged pipe support bracket on the pipe rack above Unit 100. Access
is by mobile elevating work platform from the west road. The work involves grinding and
welding at the bracket location for an estimated duration of one shift.

## 2. Hazards identified

- Ignition of flammable material by welding sparks and grinding
- Work at height from the elevating platform
- Hot surfaces and welding fume at the work position

## 3. Precautions

| # | Precaution |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fire watch posted for the duration of the hot work and for 30 minutes after completion |
| 2 | Combustible material removed or covered within the spark-fall zone below the work |
| 3 | Fire extinguisher staged at the work position and at ground level |
| 4 | Gas test of the work area completed prior to start of work — reading 0% LEL |
| 5 | Welding equipment inspected before use |
| 6 | Harness worn and attached in the elevating platform |
| 7 | Area barriered and signed at ground level below the work |

## 4. Work party

The work will be carried out by the site maintenance welding crew, with the area
operator informed before the work starts. The crew hold current site inductions and
trade qualifications for the welding activity.

## 5. Completion

On completion of the weld, the fire watch remains in place for the post-work watch
period, the area is inspected, tools and equipment are removed, and the permit is
returned to the control room for close-out.
