### Board of Directors — Health, Safety & Environment

---

## 1. The board reading

FY2025 was a strong year for safety outcomes. Our recordable and lost-time injury rates
both fell year-on-year, continuing the downward trajectory of the last three years. The
organisation invested in leadership safety tours and near-miss reporting, and the culture
metrics moved in the right direction.

## 2. Lagging indicators — outcomes (with readings)

Rates computed on OSHA-standard bases (recordable/DART ×200,000 hours; LTIFR ×1,000,000
hours) from the period case data.

| Indicator | FY2025 | FY2024 | Year-on-year | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TRIR** | **2.07** | 2.31 | ▼ ~10% | A genuine improvement on a stable-to-lower recordable count. |
| **LTIFR** | **4.44** | 6.00 | ▼ ~26% | The strongest lagging result in the paper. |

**So what:** the outcome side of the ledger is favourable and moving in the right direction.

## 3. Leading indicators — what shapes the next outcome (with readings)

| Indicator | FY2025 | Target | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit / inspection closure | 81% | 90% | Improving, 9 points short of target. |
| Near-miss reporting rate | ▲ +30% YoY | — | A maturing reporting culture. |
| Leadership safety tours | ▲ +33% YoY | — | Strong leadership visibility. |

**So what:** the leading picture supports continued improvement into FY2026.

## 4. Material risks carried into FY2026

- Work-at-height and contractor-managed lifting remain our highest-energy activities.
- Audit-closure shortfall means some corrective actions remain open into the new year.

## 5. Decisions requested of the Board

1. **Note** the improved FY2025 outcome performance.
2. **Approve** continued investment in the leadership safety-tour programme.

## 6. Outlook — FY2026 trajectory

On current trends, we expect the downward rate trajectory to continue. Management is
confident the safety system is maturing and will report quarterly against these indicators.
