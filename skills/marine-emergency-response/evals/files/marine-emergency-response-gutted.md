# Marine Emergency Response — Installation Note

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026

---

## Overview

This note sets out the emergency response arrangements for the offshore installation, covering
the main emergency scenarios and how personnel will respond.

## Response

On the general alarm, all personnel proceed to their muster stations. The OIM takes charge of
the response. Personnel are accounted for at muster and briefed on the situation.

If the situation cannot be controlled, personnel prepare to evacuate the installation using the
survival craft.

## Review

The arrangements are reviewed periodically and exercised in drills.
