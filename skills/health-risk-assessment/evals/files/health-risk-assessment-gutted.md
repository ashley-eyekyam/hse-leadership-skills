# Health Risk Assessment — Solvent Wipe-Down Line, Site-F

**Document date:** 5 July 2026
**Prepared for:** Production Manager (role)

## 1. Similar exposure groups

Two **Similar Exposure Groups (SEGs)** were defined for the wipe-down line, grouped by **task** and **role**:

- **SEG A** — line operators performing the manual solvent wipe-down.
- **SEG B** — the packing **role** at the end of the line, an adjacent **exposure group** with incidental vapour exposure.

## 2. Exposure vs limit

For SEG A, the solvent-vapour **exposure** was compared against the published **OEL**. The result for SEG A sat below the OEL; SEG B was judged materially lower given the distance from the wipe station.

## 3. Result

On the comparison above, the current arrangement for SEG A is assessed as tolerable, and the packing SEG as low.

*A working assessment for the named line.*
