# Standard Operating Procedure — Manual Print-Head Changeover, Press 4

**SOP id:** SOP-PRESS4-011 · **Reader:** press operator

## Procedure

Follow these steps to change the print head on Press 4 during a planned maintenance window:

1. Halt the press at the end of the run.
2. Open the print-head access cover.
3. Release the print-head clamps.
4. Withdraw the old print head and set it on the trolley.
5. Fit the new print head and secure the clamps.
6. Close the access cover.
7. Restart the press and run a test sheet.

## PPE

Wear the following at all times during this procedure:

- Safety gloves
- Safety glasses
- Safety boots

*Decision-support only; a competent person must review before use.*
