## 4. Control measures

The following precautions apply to this entry:

- Confined-space entry permit to be issued before the entry commences.
- Gas testing of the tank atmosphere prior to entry (O2 and LEL) using a calibrated portable monitor.
- A ventilation fan to be run at the manway for the duration of the entry.
- Standby attendant positioned at the manway maintaining continuous contact with the entrant.
- Supplied-air breathing apparatus available for the entrant.
- Full-body harness with retrieval line to be worn inside the tank.
- Entry crew briefed on the task before starting; the contractor crew to be supervised at all times.
- Hot work prohibited in the vicinity during the entry.
- Area around the manway kept clear; barriers and signage in place.

With these precautions in place the entry can proceed safely.
