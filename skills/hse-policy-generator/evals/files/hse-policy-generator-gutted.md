# Occupational Health & Safety Policy

**AcmeCo Ltd** · Issue 1 · 5 July 2026

## Policy statement

AcmeCo Ltd is committed to the highest standards of occupational health and safety. This policy
sets out our commitment to our workforce, our customers, and the communities in which we
operate.

We are committed to:

- Providing a framework for setting our health-and-safety **objectives**;
- Fulfilling our **legal** and other obligations relating to health and safety;
- The **continual improvement** of our health-and-safety management system;
- The **consultation** and participation of our workers in health-and-safety matters;
- Providing the information, instruction, training, and supervision our people need to work
  safely.

## Communication

This policy is **communicated** to all employees, is available on the company intranet, and
forms part of the induction for new starters.
