# ISO 45001 Gap Analysis — AcmeCo Plant 2

**Prepared:** 5 July 2026
**Scope:** Readiness assessment for initial certification

---

## Summary

AcmeCo Plant 2 has made good progress toward ISO 45001 certification. This assessment reviews
the management system against the standard and highlights the main areas to strengthen.

## Findings

The organisation has a health and safety policy in place and conducts regular risk assessments.
Training records are maintained and there is a programme of workplace inspections. Overall the
system is reasonably mature.

The main areas to develop are:

- Internal audit — the audit programme is still being established.
- Management review — reviews are held but could be more structured.
- Document control — some procedures are due for update.

Each area is rated on a simple maturity scale from developing to established, and most sit in the
middle of that range.

## Recommendations

The organisation should continue to build out its internal audit programme, formalise its
management reviews, and keep its procedures current. With this work the plant should be well
placed for certification.
