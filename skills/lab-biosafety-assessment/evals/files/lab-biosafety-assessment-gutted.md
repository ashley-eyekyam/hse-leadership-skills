# Biosafety Assessment — Molecular Diagnostics Bench, Site-A

**Document date:** 5 July 2026
**Prepared for:** Laboratory Manager (role)
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Scope

This assessment covers the routine handling of a bacterial isolate on the molecular-diagnostics bench, including sample receipt, resuspension and centrifugation, and downstream nucleic-acid extraction.

## 2. Agent risk group

The organism in scope is assessed as **risk group RG2** on the basis of its known transmissibility and the availability of effective treatment. Where an isolate arrives without a confirmed identification, its risk group is recorded as **[GAP]** pending identification and is not assumed.

## 3. Biosafety level

Work is assigned to **BSL-2**. The assignment reflects both the agent's RG2 classification and the fact that the bench **procedure** includes an aerosol-generating step — the isolate is resuspended and **centrifuged**, which raises the **aerosol** potential above simple open-bench manipulation.

## 4. Practices

- Good microbiological practice at the bench.
- Gloves and a laboratory coat are worn for all manipulations.
- Bench surfaces are wiped down with a disinfectant at the end of each session.
