# Occupational Health & Safety — Annual ESG Disclosure

**Acme Plc · FY2025 (1 January – 31 December 2025)** · 5 July 2026

## Our safety performance

Acme Plc is committed to protecting the health and safety of its people and to transparent
reporting of its safety performance. This section presents our headline occupational
health-and-safety metrics for the financial year, prepared with reference to **GRI 403**
(Occupational Health and Safety).

## Headline figures

| Metric | FY2025 |
|--------|--------|
| **TRIR** (Total Recordable Incident Rate) | 2.1 |
| **LTIFR** (Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate) | 0.9 |
| Fatalities | 0 |

Our recordable incident rate improved on the prior year, reflecting sustained investment in
safety leadership, training, and engagement across the business.

## Our approach

Safety is overseen at board level. During the year we continued to roll out our behavioural
safety programme, strengthened contractor management, and expanded our safety-leadership
training. We report against **GRI 403** as part of our commitment to recognised sustainability
frameworks.

## Looking ahead

We will continue to drive improvement in our safety performance and to develop our disclosure
in line with evolving good practice.
