## 1. Summary

Process safety performance at the olefins facility remained broadly stable in Q2. The
facility recorded two Tier 1 process safety events and three Tier 2 events in the
quarter, with the Tier 1 rate for the quarter at 0.28 and the Tier 2 rate at 0.42. The
events were investigated and closed through the site's incident process.

| # | Month | Event | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Commentary

The Q2 events were concentrated in the cracked-gas and quench systems, and the
engineering team has inspection activity planned in both areas for Q3. Overall the
facility's performance this quarter is consistent with recent periods, and the
quarter-on-quarter picture remains stable.

The site leadership team reviewed the Q2 performance at the monthly review, and the
performance summary above has been included in the quarterly report to the divisional
safety committee.
