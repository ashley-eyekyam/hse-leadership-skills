# Behavioural Safety Observation Programme — Order-Picking Crew, Leeds DC

**Programme design** · 5 July 2026

## 1. Purpose

This behavioural safety observation programme is designed for the order-picking operation at
the Leeds distribution centre. It aims to raise safety performance by observing how work is
done and by giving people timely, constructive **feedback** on what is seen.

## 2. Observation card

Observers use a card built around **observable**, **site-specific** behaviours tied to the
picking task. Example **card items**:

- Three points of contact when climbing the pick-ladder
- Body positioned out of the line of fire when cutting banding
- Housekeeping maintained in the pick-aisle
- Correct manual-handling posture when lifting from the lower pick-face

Each item describes a **behaviour** that can be seen during a short observation on the pick
line.

## 3. The conversation

Observation is a **two-way** conversation. The observer shares what was seen, listens to the
picker's own account, and gives immediate **feedback**. The tone is supportive and focused on
learning.

## 4. Metrics

The programme tracks:

- **Percent-safe** — the proportion of observed behaviours that were safe
- **Participation** — how many observations are being completed

These are reported on the site safety dashboard so trends can be seen over time.

## 5. Roll-out

The programme will be rolled out to the picking crew with a short briefing so everyone
understands its purpose.
