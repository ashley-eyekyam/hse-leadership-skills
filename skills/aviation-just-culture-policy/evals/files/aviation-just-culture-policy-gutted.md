# Just Culture Policy

**DGCA-certificated A320 scheduled passenger operator** · 5 July 2026

## 1. Our commitment

The operator is committed to a **just culture** in which staff are treated fairly and
consistently when safety matters arise. A just culture underpins our confidential and
mandatory safety reporting and our wider safety management system.

## 2. The culpability ladder

Our **decision tree** is based on a **culpability** ladder that recognises that most safety
events involve **honest error** rather than blameworthy conduct. The ladder distinguishes:

- **Honest error** — an unintended slip, lapse, or mistake made while trying to do the right
  thing.
- **At-risk** behaviour — a choice where the risk was not recognised or was believed justified.
- **Reckless** conduct — a conscious disregard of a substantial and unjustifiable risk.
- **Negligent** conduct — falling below the standard expected of a reasonable person.

## 3. Application

Where a safety event occurs, management considers where the individual's actions sit on the
ladder and responds proportionately, consistent with a just culture.

## 4. Legal basis

The policy is drafted consistent with the safety-data principles of ICAO Annex 19.
