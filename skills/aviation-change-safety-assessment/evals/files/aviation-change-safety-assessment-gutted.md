# Change Safety Assessment — Addition of a Hot-and-High Destination

**DGCA-certificated A320 scheduled operator** · Change ref: CSA-2026-014 · Dated: 5 July 2026

## 1. The change

The operator proposes to add a new hot-and-high destination (aerodrome elevation ~6,500 ft,
high ambient temperatures) to the scheduled A320 network. This assessment considers the safety
of that change before it takes effect.

## 2. New hazards identified

The following **new hazards** are introduced by operating into this environment:

| # | Hazard | Severity | Likelihood | Risk |
|---|--------|----------|------------|------|
| H1 | Unstabilised approach into terrain-constrained approach | Hazardous | Occasional | High |
| H2 | Reduced climb performance on engine-out in high density-altitude | Hazardous | Remote | Medium |
| H3 | Reduced runway margins in high density-altitude | Major | Occasional | Medium |

Each hazard has been scored on the operator's risk scheme using its severity and likelihood.

## 3. Regulatory basis

The assessment is framed against the applicable DGCA requirements for network changes. The
exact CAR reference is to be confirmed by the postholder [GAP].

## 4. Controls

A crew briefing note covering the destination's approach profile and performance
considerations will be issued to flight crews before the destination goes live. Crews will be
made aware of the specific characteristics of the aerodrome.

## 5. Conclusion

The change introduces the hazards listed above, which have been assessed. With the crew
briefing in place, the operation into the new destination can proceed.
