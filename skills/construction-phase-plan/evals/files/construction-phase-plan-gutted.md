# Construction Phase Plan — Meridian Court roof-replacement & recladding

**Project:** Meridian Court — strip and replace the flat roof and recladding to a four-storey
residential block, works to the front and rear elevations. **Construction phase:** weeks 3–14.
**Prepared:** 5 July 2026. **Jurisdiction:** UK — CDM 2015.

## 1. Project description & programme
The works proceed as roof strip-out → deck repair → new membrane → recladding of the front and
rear elevations. The programme runs across weeks 3–14 with a scaffold erected to full height for
access. The block remains tenanted throughout the works.

## 2. Management & arrangements
| Duty-holder | Responsibility | Arrangement |
|---|---|---|
| Principal Contractor | Overall site management | Daily coordination meeting; weekly progress review |
| Site Manager | Day-to-day supervision | Site diary; toolbox talks each Monday |
| Scaffold Contractor | Access provision | Scaffold inspected weekly and tag-signed |

- Site induction is delivered to every operative before first access.
- Consultation is through the Monday toolbox talk and the site noticeboard.

## 3. Site rules
- Hard hats, hi-vis and safety footwear mandatory across the whole site.
- The scaffold is not to be altered except by the scaffold contractor.
- Deliveries are booked in and marshalled at the front gate.

## 4. Significant risks & controls by activity
| Activity | Significant hazard | Control | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Roof strip-out at height | Fall from the roof edge | Operatives to wear full-body harnesses clipped to the scaffold and be trained in their use; toe-boards to the scaffold lift | Site Manager | 12 Jul 2026 |
| Recladding from the scaffold | Fall / dropped materials | Harnesses worn at the working lift; debris netting to the scaffold; helmets below | Works Supervisor | 15 Jul 2026 |
| Material hoisting | Struck by falling load | Hoist operated by a trained banksman; area kept clear during lifts | Site Manager | 12 Jul 2026 |

All operatives are briefed to wear their harnesses at all times when on the roof or the upper
scaffold lifts, and supervision monitors compliance daily.

## 5. Welfare arrangements
Welfare is provided for the site from the first week: toilets, hot-water washing, drinking water,
and a heated rest and drying room, maintained through the construction phase.

## 6. Review schedule
This plan is a live document and carries a review schedule — it is reviewed on a change of method
or sequence and at each key phase transition through the works.
